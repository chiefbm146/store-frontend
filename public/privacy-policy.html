🌊 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2025

1. Introduction

AARIE.CA ("we," "us," or "our") is a platform that builds and hosts websites for our clients ("Merchants"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services or make a purchase from a Merchant's website hosted by us.

2. Information We Collect

As the platform owner, we collect and store the following information on behalf of our Merchants:

  • For Customers of Merchants: Name, Email, Phone Number, and order details. This information is collected to process orders and for communication between you and the Merchant.
  • For our Merchants: Business information required to set up your store and your Stripe Connect Express account.
  • Payment Information: All payments are processed securely through Stripe. We do not see or store your credit card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To enable order fulfillment and communication between Customers and Merchants.
  • To process payments via Stripe.
  • To provide our website building and hosting services to Merchants.
  • To improve our platform and prevent fraud.

4. Data Storage & Security

All personal and transactional data is stored in secure, encrypted databases on Google Cloud Firestore. We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption and role-based access controls, to protect your information.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Both Merchants and their customers have the right to request the deletion of their personal data.

  • Request Deletion: Use our secure portal to submit a data deletion request

We will respond to data deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Services

We share your information only with:

  • Stripe: For payment processing. Our platform uses Stripe Connect, and our Merchants are onboarded with Stripe Express accounts.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law.
