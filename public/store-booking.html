Frequently Asked Questions

How is it possible to build a custom site in just 7-10 days?

We've pre-built a powerful and secure backend infrastructure on Google Cloud. Instead of starting from scratch every time, we configure our existing, battle-tested system with your unique branding, products, and AI personality. The heavy lifting is already done.

Will I actually own my website?

You 100% own what matters most: your brand, your logo, all of your content, and your customer data. The underlying code and AI platform that power your site are proprietary to AARIE.CA. We provide this technology to you as a service. This model allows us to handle all the complex security, maintenance, and performance updates for you, so you never have to worry about the technical side.

Why is this better than a template from Wix or Shopify?

Templates look generic and are slow. We custom-code your site, making it faster, more unique, and better for SEO. Most importantly, you get a custom AI assistant trained specifically on your business, something no template builder can offer.

Can I update the site myself?

Not directly, and that's by design. We provide a fully managed service to ensure your site remains fast, secure, and professional. You have a dedicated technical partner—simply email us the changes you need (like updating a product price or adding a new photo), and if it falls within the scope of your plan, we'll handle it for you. Future plans will include a self-service dashboard.

Is my customer data safe?

Absolutely. We use Google Cloud for hosting and Stripe for payments—both are industry leaders in security. Your customers' payment information never touches our servers.
