Healing from the Land, Rooted in Tradition

Welcome to the world of Sxexet Spath (David Peters), a dedicated medicine maker and land steward from the Lower Mainland, British Columbia. Drawing upon generations of Stó:lō knowledge, he crafts potent remedies from the natural world, honoring the sacred relationship between healer, plant, and spirit.

Explore Medicines

Sxexet Spath's hands are guided by generations of Stó:lō knowledge and a lifetime spent learning from the land. He spends countless hours in the forests, riverbanks, and mountains of his traditional territory, understanding the subtle rhythms of the seasons, knowing where plants thrive, and honoring the ancient protocols for respectful harvesting.

His medicine-making practice is rooted in the understanding that true healing encompasses not only the physical body but also the spirit and mind. Every remedy is infused with intention and care, transforming the bounty of the land into powerful expressions of gratitude and respect for the Creator's gifts.

Explore this site to discover medicines crafted with ancestral wisdom, sustainable practices, and a profound commitment to the wellness of both people and the land.

The Art of Medicine Making

From plant to remedy, with reverence and care

The traditional practice of Stó:lō medicine making is a sacred trust - a conversation between the healer, the plant, and the ancestors. For the Stó:lō people, the land is not merely a resource but a living relative, a provider, and a teacher. Sxexet Spath learned to listen to the plants, to understand their properties, and to feel the healing waiting within each remedy.

Salves & Topical Medicines

Infused oils and waxes that are applied directly to skin. Devil's Club, plantain, and yarrow are crafted into healing balms for wounds, inflammation, and skin conditions.

Tinctures & Extracts

Alcohol-based preparations that extract and preserve the medicinal compounds of plants. Taken internally, tinctures support long-term wellness and deep healing.

Teas & Infusions

Dried herbs steeped in hot water to create gentle, nourishing remedies. Perfect for daily wellness, immune support, and comforting ritual.

Syrups & Tonics

Concentrated preparations of medicinal plants combined with honey or other carriers. Particularly potent for respiratory health and seasonal immune support.

Poultices & Decoctions

Fresh plant material prepared through heating and mashing, applied to the body for acute conditions. Traditional methods for drawing out infection and promoting healing.

The Healer's Path

Rooted in Tradition, Healing for Tomorrow

Sxexet Spath's knowledge comes not from academic study, but from a deeper, more intimate source: his community, his Elders, the teachings passed down through generations, and countless hours spent directly with the plants and the land itself.

This path of learning has instilled in him an authentic voice and a profound connection to the living pharmacy of the Stó:lō territory. From a young age, he was drawn to the healing arts—learning to recognize plants by sight and scent, understanding their seasonal cycles, and honoring the protocols of respectful gathering that ensure the plants' survival for future generations.

Sxexet Spath is more than a medicine maker; he is a culture bearer, a land steward, and a bridge between ancestral wisdom and contemporary wellness. His work is a living, breathing expression of Stó:lō identity and commitment to the health of both people and the land.
