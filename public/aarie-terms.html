🏪 AARIE.CA Affiliate Program Terms

Effective Date: November 27, 2025

Welcome to the AARIE.CA Affiliate Program

Thank you for your interest in becoming an affiliate partner with AARIE.CA. This document outlines the terms and conditions of the AARIE.CA Affiliate Program. By submitting an affiliate application and being approved, you agree to all terms outlined below.

1. Commission Structure

Standard Affiliate Commission

As an approved affiliate, you will earn an 8% commission on every completed sale made by a customer who clicked your unique affiliate link.

Commission Calculation Example: If a customer signs up for a service or product worth $1,000 through your affiliate link, you earn $80 in commission.

Commissions apply to all products and services offered through AARIE.CA, including consultations, setup fees, and premium services.

2. Payment Methods & Timing

Payment Options

We offer flexible payment options to suit your preferences:

  • Stripe Connect: (Recommended) Automatic payouts directly to your connected Stripe account. Fastest and most transparent option with real-time tracking through your Stripe dashboard.
  • E-Transfer: Canadian affiliates can receive commissions via e-transfer to a registered email address.
  • PayPal: Receive payments to your PayPal account.

Payment Timing

Commissions are paid out monthly, calculated from the 1st to the last day of each calendar month. Payouts are processed within 10 business days of the end of the month in which sales were completed.

There is no minimum commission threshold required for payment. Even if you earn $1 in a month, you will receive it.

3. Tracking & Attribution

We use a simple, transparent link-based tracking system:

  • Your unique affiliate link contains a ?ref=yourname parameter
  • When a customer clicks your link and completes a purchase in the same session or within a 30-day cookie window, that sale is attributed to you
  • You can track all your referrals and earnings through your affiliate dashboard
  • Your responsibility: It is your responsibility to share your correct unique affiliate link and track your own promotions

4. Professional Conduct & Brand Representation

We expect all affiliates to represent AARIE.CA professionally and ethically:

  • No Spam: Do not send unsolicited emails or messages promoting your affiliate link
  • No Misleading Advertising: Accurately represent AARIE.CA's services. Do not make false claims about features, benefits, or earnings potential
  • Honest Promotion: Be transparent that you are an affiliate and may earn commission from referrals
  • No Paid Ads: Do not purchase paid search ads (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, etc.) targeting your own affiliate link
  • Respect Brand Guidelines: Use AARIE.CA branding appropriately and respectfully
  • No Illegal Activity: Do not promote through illegal means or violate any laws or regulations

Failure to comply with these standards may result in immediate termination of your affiliate account without compensation.

5. Refunds & Chargebacks

AARIE.CA offers refund protections to customers. If a customer who clicked your link receives a refund, initiates a chargeback, or disputes the charge:

  • Your commission for that sale is reversed or withheld
  • If you've already been paid, we will deduct the commission amount from your next payout
  • This is tracked automatically through our payment processor

6. Age & Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for the AARIE.CA Affiliate Program:

  • You must be 18 years of age or older
  • You must have a valid email address and contact information
  • You must provide accurate information in your application
  • You must not have been previously removed from the program for violation of terms

7. Program Modifications & Termination

Changes to Terms

AARIE.CA reserves the right to modify commission rates, payment terms, and program rules with 30 days written notice. Commissions already earned under previous terms are grandfathered and remain valid.

Account Termination

AARIE.CA may terminate an affiliate's account at any time for:

  • Violation of this terms agreement
  • Fraudulent or deceptive practices
  • Extended inactivity (no referrals for 12+ months)
  • Legal or regulatory issues

8. Intellectual Property & Privacy

You may not use AARIE.CA trademarks, logos, or proprietary content without explicit written permission. All customer data remains the property of AARIE.CA and must be treated as confidential.

9. Limitation of Liability

AARIE.CA makes no guarantees about earnings or customer behavior. You assume all risk related to your promotion methods. AARIE.CA is not responsible for lost earnings due to technical issues, system downtime, or customer refunds.

By submitting your affiliate application, you confirm that:
  • You are 18 years of age or older
  • You have read and understood these terms
  • You agree to comply with all terms outlined above
  • You will promote AARIE.CA professionally and ethically
